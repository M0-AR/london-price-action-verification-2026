🌱 Beginner guide — from zero to dangerous (in a good way)

Let's work this out in a step-by-step way to be sure we have the right answer. No prior knowledge assumed.

0 · What are we trading?

GBPJPY = yen per pound. Pip = 0.01 (yen pairs) or 0.0001 (most others). Gold jumps harder per tick — that's why beginners start with FX.

1 · When does it move?

Asia: quiet. London 06:00–10:00 UTC: busiest for pound/yen. New York afternoon: gold's peak. London's 15-min wiggle here is 1.27–1.52× Asia's.

2 · What is price action?

Read the candle (open/high/low/close). Bottom wick = buyers stepped in. Support = floor that held before. Clean-left (LTA) = clear path on the left. Rule needs all + a fully closed 15-min candle.

3 · The only math: risk

$10,000 paper → risk 1% ($100)/trade, stop = "I'm wrong", target = 2× risk. Five losses ≈ −5%, alive. At 5% risk: −17.5%, 27% drawdown. No stop looks great — until one news candle erases half.

4 · Patience is a position

Pros here take 2–3 trades/week and skip the rest. Early (FOMO) entries "won" in one 60-day window — small-sample noise with hidden tail risk.

5 · Read results like a pro

Always ask: n (how many trades?), costs on? every quarter consistent? interval crossing zero? If a seller hides these, walk away.
